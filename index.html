⚡ How Decay Mode Works

  • 1. Each player can only hold 3 active marks on the grid at any time.
  • 2. Once you have 3 marks down, your oldest mark begins pulsing.
  • 3. Placing your 4th mark evaporates that oldest mark, opening the square back up!
  • 4. Align 3 marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win. Draws are impossible!
